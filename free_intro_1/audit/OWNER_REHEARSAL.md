# Written presentation rehearsal

These are prepared words using the fictional businesses and the actual final saved records. No owner was interviewed and no external message was sent.

## Opening

“I connect the repeated handoffs between the tools a business uses. Here is the original input, here are the separate places that need it, and here are the saved records after one reviewed intake. I can also show an interrupted handoff and its recovery.”

“In this fictional Desert Air example, the workflow saved records in Customer Profiles, Dispatch Desk, Billing Desk. Each has its own stored ID. Let’s open those records rather than relying only on a success banner.”

Observed results: Customer Profiles: 1 saved records, selected record 873272c2-7bc, status Active customer; Dispatch Desk: 2 saved records, selected record 8e956804-740, status Completed; Billing Desk: 2 saved records, selected record cb49a5b1-b7b, status Draft.

Ask the owner: “Where do you retype these facts today? What identifies the same job or person? Who approves changes, and what should happen when a destination cannot accept an update?”

Technical follow-up: “The local connector writes through an authenticated API, reads the result back, and compares the saved values. Your actual product still needs its own supported interface, permission, mapping, and tests.”

## Cost language

“This showcase has no required paid service charges on the existing computer. A real deployment has separate implementation, hosting, vendor, and support choices. Field counts are not measured labor savings, and the demo is not a production capacity benchmark.”
